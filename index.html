Engagement Models

Two ways to build. One standard of excellence.

Choose the level of ownership that fits your stage, budget, and long-term plans. You can start with one model and move to the other as you grow.

MOST POPULAR

Partnership Model

Build · Operate · Transfer (BOT) / Co-Owned GCC

We build and run your GCC alongside you. Your leaders shape culture and technical direction, while we handle the entity, infrastructure, compliance, and operations. When you're ready, we transfer the center, including its people, assets, and processes, into your own Indian entity.

  • Shared governance with joint steering committee
  • Your brand, your culture, your engineering practices
  • Option to take full ownership in 18–36 months
  • Transparent cost-plus pricing with no hidden markups
  • Pre-agreed transfer terms and a smooth legal transition
  • We stay on as advisors after the transfer, if you want us to
Best for: Firms that want a long-term strategic asset in India and eventual full ownership, without the risk of setting it up alone.

Managed / Outsourced Model

Dedicated Offshore Development Center (ODC)

We provide a fully managed, dedicated team that works exclusively on your products under our legal entity. You direct the work. We take care of hiring, HR, payroll, facilities, IT, security, and day-to-day delivery management.

  • No Indian entity or local investment required
  • Predictable monthly per-resource pricing
  • Fastest path to a productive team
  • Dedicated delivery manager and SLAs
  • Flexible ramp-up and ramp-down terms
  • Option to convert to a BOT partnership later
Best for: Firms that want to add capacity quickly, test the offshore model, or keep a simple operating structure.

FAQ

Common questions

How is a GCC different from traditional outsourcing?

With outsourcing, a vendor delivers projects using its own shared staff. With a GCC, you get a dedicated team that works only on your products, follows your processes, and builds lasting knowledge of your business. It functions as an extension of your company, and under the partnership model it can eventually become fully yours.

What is the minimum team size to get started?

Under the managed model, you can start with a pod of about five engineers. For a Build-Operate-Transfer partnership, we usually recommend planning for 15 or more engineers over the first year so the economics of your own entity make sense.

Do I need to set up an Indian company?

Not for the managed model. The team works under our entity. For the partnership model, we operate the center under our entity at first and then help you form your own Indian subsidiary when it's time to transfer.

How is my intellectual property protected?

All work product and IP are assigned to you by contract. Every team member signs confidentiality and IP assignment agreements, and we use dedicated, access-controlled environments, device management, and audit logging aligned with standards such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001.

Can I interview and approve the engineers?

Yes. We source and screen candidates, and you take part in final interviews and approve every hire. Many clients also send leaders to India during the launch to help set the culture.

How do you handle attrition?

Through market-competitive pay, clear career paths, meaningful product work, and strong engagement programs. If someone does leave, we manage backfill and knowledge continuity at no extra recruiting cost to you.

What does it cost?

Cost depends on team size, role mix, location, and engagement model. The managed model uses a simple monthly rate per resource. The partnership model uses open-book cost-plus pricing. We'll prepare a detailed total cost of ownership estimate during our first strategy session.
